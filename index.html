Training Applicants Dashboard

An interactive Power BI report that lets management explore about 2,900 training applicants by university, specialization, and GPA scale. Below are screenshots of the finished report in use.

What it answers

  • Which universities and specializations do applicants come from?
  • How do GPAs look across the three scales used (4, 5, and 100 points)?
  • What does any slice of applicants look like when filtered?

My role

  • Worked on it individually during my SDAIA cooperative training.
  • Cleaned and validated the data; standardized GPA scales into one percentage.
  • Built the measures, charts, and slicers, then presented it to management and refined the design after their feedback.

Tools

Microsoft Excel, Power BI (Power Query, DAX measures).

Screenshots show aggregated counts only; no individual applicant data is shown or published. The average GPA combines three scales after conversion to a percentage, so it is an overview figure. The largest specialization group is “Other”.
